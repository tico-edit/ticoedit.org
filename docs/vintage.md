## Retro and vintage computers

Small versions of **tico** have been re-implemented for
vintage computers.  The goal is to preserve the muscle
memory of common keystrokes without porting all of the
features that won't fit in the memory of these older
computers or run well on the older CPUs.

* [tico16](tico16) - **tico** for 16 Bit MS-DOS.  Works on 8086 and later.
* [tico68](tico68) - **tico** for Moterola 68000 Mac.  Works on Mac Plus and later.
